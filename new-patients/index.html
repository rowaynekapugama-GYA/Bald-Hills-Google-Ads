About the practice

A family-run practice that makes the dentist a place you want to go.

Bald Hills Dental is led by Dr Emad Hanna, who has more than 25 years of experience, alongside a team of dentists, a prosthetist and friendly support staff. It's a modern clinic at 18 Bald Hills Road with free onsite parking at the door.

Whether you're new to the area or simply ready for a change, we'll take the time to get to know you, explain everything clearly and never rush you.

Our services

Everything your family needs, under one roof.

From a routine check-up to an urgent repair, most care happens right here in Bald Hills without referrals across town.

Check-ups and cleans

Thorough exams, scale and clean, and advice to keep teeth healthy.

Children's dentistry

Gentle, fun visits that help kids feel at home in the chair.

Fillings and tooth decay

Tooth-coloured fillings that repair decay and blend in.

Emergency dental

Same-day care for toothache, broken teeth and swelling.

Root canal treatment

Saving an infected tooth and easing the pain.

Wisdom teeth

Assessment with 3D imaging and removal when needed.

Crowns and bridges

Strong, natural-looking repairs designed digitally.

Dentures

Custom dentures made by our in-house prosthetist.

Gum disease

Treatment to stop bleeding gums and protect your teeth.

Invisalign and orthodontics

Straighter teeth with clear aligners or braces.

Snoring and sleep apnoea

Dental appliances that may help you sleep better.

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening for a brighter smile.
